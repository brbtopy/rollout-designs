# Commission brief — RollOut tow-type illustrations

## What I need
**Four matching flat-vector illustrations, one per tow-truck type.** They must read
as one consistent set. They appear as small cards a stranded driver scrolls
through to choose how their vehicle gets towed, so clarity at small size and a
consistent look across the four matter more than fine detail.

## About the app
RollOut is a tow-truck ride-hailing app — think Uber, but for tow trucks —
operating in Accra, Ghana. When a driver breaks down, they open the app and pick
the kind of tow they need. These four illustrations are that picker.

## The four subjects — mechanical accuracy is the whole job
A stranded driver has to recognise the right truck, so each one must be
mechanically correct. The flatbed-vs-wheel-lift difference especially must be
unmistakable — this is the detail most illustrators get wrong.

1. **Flatbed (rollback)** — a truck with a flat, tilting deck; a car sits fully
   ON the deck with all four wheels on the platform. The classic "car carried
   flat" image.
2. **Wheel-lift** — a truck towing a car by an under-lift/yoke that cradles and
   lifts TWO of the car's wheels off the ground; the car's other two wheels stay
   ON the road, so the car is towed at an angle (nose-up or tail-up). This is
   **not** a flatbed and **not** an old hook-and-chain. Please get this one right
   — reference photo attached.
3. **Heavy-duty** — a large, heavy wrecker for towing big vehicles (trucks,
   buses, other tow trucks): bigger cab, more axles, an integrated boom/crane. It
   should clearly read as bigger and more industrial than the others.
4. **Other / Not sure** — a neutral, friendly, generic tow truck for the "I'm not
   sure which I need" choice. Simple and approachable; no specific mechanism
   emphasised.

Reference photos of the exact flatbed and wheel-lift mechanisms are attached with
this brief. If the wheel-lift is unclear, please ask before starting — it's the
make-or-break detail.

## Style
- **Flat vector illustration** — clean outlines, flat fills, minimal detail; in
  the spirit of Storyset / unDraw / Freepik "flat" illustrations. (Style
  references attached.)
- **Subject only** — just the truck (plus a generic car where it clarifies the
  type, i.e. flatbed and wheel-lift). **No** background scenery, **no** people,
  **no** city. A soft ground shadow is welcome. These are icon-illustrations, not
  full scenes.
- **Consistent as a set** — identical across all four: same side-profile viewing
  angle, same facing direction, same line weight, same light source, same level
  of detail, same ground-shadow treatment, same relative scale and eye-level.
  They must sit side by side and obviously belong together.
- **Legible at ~120–160 px wide** — that's the real display size. Avoid detail
  that muddies when small.
- *Optional:* if you'd rather do a subtle isometric / soft-3D take than flat
  side-profile, that's welcome — send one sample first so we confirm the look
  before you do all four. The palette and "as a set" rules still apply.

## Palette — please match exactly
The app is warm and near-monochrome. Keep the illustrations mostly muted and use
yellow only as a small accent, because the app uses yellow elsewhere.

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Ink / outlines | `#141414` |
| Warm off-white (negative space) | `#F5F4F0` |
| Truck & car bodies (use 2–3 for depth) | `#D4D1C9`, `#B8B4AC`, `#8A8780` |
| Accent — beacon light / hazard stripe only, sparingly | `#FFDE15` |

Please avoid other saturated colours (no bright reds, blues or greens). The set
should feel calm and warm.

## Deliverables
- **Editable vector source**: SVG (preferred) or AI/EPS — layered and clean, so
  colours can be adjusted later.
- **Exported PNG**: transparent background, at 1×, 2× and 3× (target 3× ≈ 480 px
  on the long edge).
- All four on the **same canvas size and aspect ratio** (square or 4:3), with the
  truck consistently placed and scaled.
- **Transparent background** — the app supplies the card colour.

## Licensing — required
- Full commercial rights / buyout for use in a commercial mobile app and its
  marketing: worldwide, perpetual, **no attribution required**.
- Confirmation the work is original and not built on stock that restricts this
  use.
- Editable source files handed over on delivery.
